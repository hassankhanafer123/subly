# BRAND — non-negotiables

## Audience
- General public with a lease. NOT students.
- Reasons to sublet: traveling, working remotely, internship/co-op, away for
  the summer, family/personal, studying abroad.
- Never say: school, campus, university, dorm, .edu, semester, co-op-as-school.
- Tone: confident, calm, trustworthy. Like Airbnb, not a startup landing page.

## Palette (Airbnb tokens — do not invent hex)
- Accent / Rausch: #FF385C   (ONLY accent — CTAs, active tab, heart, price)
- Accent pressed: #E00B41
- Text / Ink Black: #222222   (~90% of text)
- Secondary text: #6A6A6A
- Faint / metadata: #929292
- Canvas: #FFFFFF
- Soft section bg: #F7F7F7
- Hairline border: #DDDDDD
- Error: #C13515 · Success: #0E8A5F

## Type
- One sans family (Airbnb Cereal → system fallback). Body weight 500.
- Sizes: 11 badge · 13 caption · 14 label · 16 body · 20 listing title ·
  22 subsection · 28 page heading · 32–38 hero.
- Negative letter-spacing on large headings.

## Shape & depth
- Photo rounding 14–20px. Circular icon buttons (heart, back, close).
- Shadows: soft and low, or none. No neumorphism, no glassmorphism, no gradients
  as surfaces (coral→magenta gradient only on the wordmark, if at all).

## Rules
- One accent element per screen. Everything else is ink + grayscale.
- Full-bleed real photography is the primary visual vocabulary.
- Restraint. If a screen has more than one coral moment, cut one.
